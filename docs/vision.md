# CCVM Vision

CCVM is built for MSPs, IT service providers and security teams that need a unified operational view of assets, vulnerabilities and business risk.

## Principles

- Product over wrapper
- Plugin-first scanner architecture
- MSP-grade multi-customer workflows
- Simple executive reporting
- Open architecture and portable deployment

## Not in scope for v1

- Full SIEM replacement
- EDR replacement
- Ticketing system replacement
- Public SaaS multi-region architecture
